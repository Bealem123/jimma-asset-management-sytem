Photos of the front page (src/pages/Landing.jsx)

Put photographs of Jimma in this folder with exactly these names. Each one replaces the colour
gradient shown in its place; a missing photo simply leaves the gradient. Use photos the City owns
or has permission to use. Landscape JPG, about 2000 px wide and under 500 KB each, keeps the page fast.

  hero.jpg                 opening photo, shown until the hero film (YouTube) starts and whenever it cannot play
                           (no internet, reduced motion, data saver): Jimma from above, ideally at sunset
  about.png                the system shown on a mobile phone, beside the "About" text: a PNG cut out on a
                           transparent background (no frame is drawn around it, so the page shows through)
  register.jpg             panel "Register, verify, approve": an office, a municipal building or land
  machinery.jpg            panel "Inspect and maintain": a vehicle, road or machine being inspected
  map.jpg                  panel "Map, QR codes and field work": a street, a market or a Kebele
  report.jpg               panel "Accountable every month": the City Administration building
  level-mayor.jpg          first level card, now "Lemat Trufat": Prime Minister Dr. Abiy Ahmed visiting the avocado crates
  level-kifle-ketema.jpg   card "Kifle Ketema": a Kifle Ketema office or a main street of the area
  level-kebele.jpg         card "Jimma City Center": Jimma at night from above, the roundabout monument and the lit square
  level-sector.jpg         card "Sector": a sector office, or its staff with the assets they register
  closing.jpg              last full-screen panel: illustration of 13 Kebeles joined through 3 Kifle Ketemas into one
                           register (the monument photo it replaced is kept as closing.original-monument.jpg)

Each photo must show what its section talks about; a pretty photo of something else confuses
visitors. No photos of private businesses or with logos or text printed on them.

The text sits on the left of each panel and the page darkens the photo slightly, so photos whose
subject is on the right and that are not too bright work best. After adding photos, rebuild the
front end (npm run build) or simply reload the page in development.
